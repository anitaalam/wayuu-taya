Agroecology

Supporting sustainable farming practices that respect the land and empower Wayuu communities to grow their own food.

Sustainable Agriculture

Growing Together

The Agroecology program helps Wayuu communities develop sustainable agricultural practices adapted to the arid conditions of La Guajira. Through training, resources, and community gardens, families gain the skills and tools to cultivate nutritious food year-round.

By combining traditional indigenous knowledge with modern sustainable techniques, we help build food sovereignty and reduce dependence on external food sources.

Your donation helps us provide seeds, tools, and training to Wayuu farming communities.
