Wayuubags (mochilas), also known as Susu, are handwoven by Wayuu women who put all their magic, passion, and love into each piece. Each bag is done solely by one person, from beginning to end, and it takes approximately 20 days of intricate work to make each one.

No two bags are alike — slight variations in color and pattern make each one uniquely yours.

Material 100% Cotton Fiber
Craft Time ~20 days of hand-weaving
Care Hand wash or delicate cycle. Hang to dry.
